Our approach

Filling the funding gap for moderate-scale, lower-risk projects.

Many proven fields are too small for the majors and too technical for generalist investors. That is where we work.

A widening energy gap. Demand is rising with population growth, AI and data centres and the electrification of energy use, while structural underinvestment and production decline limit supply. Renewable and alternative sources cannot yet fully replace today's baseload requirements.

Our strategy. We capture oil and gas assets and opportunities in that gap. Where possible we use hybrid field power, combining oil and gas with renewables, to support a practical energy transition.

01

Proven resources

We focus on producing and appraised assets in established basins, where earlier exploration or field development has already de-risked the subsurface.

02

Capital plus expertise

We bring private investment together with hands-on geoscience and commercial experience, working closely with in-country operators and partners.

03

Maximising existing infrastructure

Restarting and optimising existing fields extends the life of valuable assets, supporting local jobs, government revenues and reliable energy supply.

What we look for

Direct title

Ownership of oil and gas reserves and resources through licence title and joint venture participation.

Non-operated equity

Equity in existing assets with established operators. We would also consider acquiring an operating company together with its team.

Accessible geography

We are based in the UK and, for operational reasons, broadly focus within eight hours of GMT.

PERU · 30% · PRODUCING

Block 8

A technical service contract in the northern Peruvian Amazon, won through a competitive bid. Once a flagship state development in the 1970s, the block is being restarted by operator Upland Oil & Gas, bringing wells back online since late 2024. It produces about 5,500 barrels of oil per day (October 2026), sold to the PetroPeru refinery in Iquitos and ATEM in Manaus. Further satellite facilities put the 6,000 barrel per day target within sight.

PERU · 30% · APPRAISAL AND DEVELOPMENT

Block XXIII

An onshore coastal block in northwest Peru, within about 10 km of the Pan-American Highway, operated by Upland Oil & Gas. Its conventional gas fields are proven by multiple wells and flow tests, and Piedra Candela is being developed to supply compressed natural gas. The Cardalitos syncline adds unconventional gas potential.

PERU · 100% · TECHNICAL EVALUATION

Block XCIX

A technical evaluation area held 100% by Tetra Energies in the Ucayali basin of the central Peruvian Amazon, near the Brazilian border. It contains the undeveloped Sheshea discovery, with further exploration upside. The current work programme is a geological and geophysical evaluation of the area: reviewing all existing well data, reprocessing the available 3D seismic and assessing the hydrocarbon potential.

UK · 5% · SHUT-IN

Avington field, PEDL070

An oil field near Winchester in the onshore Wessex basin, in subarea SU52a, operated by Star Energy. The field is shut-in and scheduled for abandonment.

UK · 25% · EXPLORATION

PL090, subareas SY78a and SY88a

An exploration interest in Dorset, operated by Egdon Resources, with potential for oil in the Sherwood and Bridport sandstones. The primary prospect, Broadmayne, is a tilted fault block defined on 3D seismic. The interest excludes the Waddock Cross field.

Our team

Founded on technical depth and commercial discipline.
